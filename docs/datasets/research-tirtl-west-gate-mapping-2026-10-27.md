# Research decision: Victoria TIRTL sites 504/505 → West Gate SUMO

**Audit date:** 2026-10-27  
**Result:** `UNSUITABLE_FOR_EMPIRICAL_VALIDATION`  
**Mapping status:** Sites 504 and 505 remain unresolved and comparison-ineligible. No SUMO edge, lane, or detector measurement section is assigned.

## Decision

No high-confidence mapping candidate can be responsibly selected from the available evidence. The live DTP site table confirms the site names and point coordinates, and Victorian sources describe the road network, but neither establishes that the published points are detector installation locations or locates the measurement line across a particular carriageway or lane group. The DTP resource does not state the coordinate reference system or positional accuracy. Its direction names are evidence of intended direction, not installation verification. Device documentation confirms that TIRTL can be configured for directional and multi-lane measurement; it does not establish how these two devices were installed or what the exported heading values mean.

The nearest-way distances recorded in the existing register remain exploratory estimates made under an unverified CRS. They are not used here to infer a segment, direction, lane, or match. New official roadway-centerline metadata does not resolve that gap: centerlines are useful network geometry, not as-built detector surveys. The SUMO network remains OSM-derived rather than engineering-validated as-built geometry.

This result preserves the current integration state: both sites are unresolved, descriptive profiling only, and ineligible for exploratory or primary observed-versus-SUMO comparison. It does not overturn or upgrade earlier reviews.

## Register fit

The current [mapping register](victoria-tirtl-mapping-register.json) correctly leaves both `mapped_segment_id` and `lane_id` null, records confidence as `unresolved`, and sets `eligible_for_exploratory_comparison` and `eligible_for_primary_validation` to false. This review does not modify those statuses. Existing candidate ways remain candidates only; no empirical comparison should be run using them.

## Upgrade evidence required

1. DTP confirmation of the coordinate CRS/datum, positional accuracy, and whether the coordinate denotes the detector receiver/transmitter or the traffic measurement section.
2. Installation/as-built drawing or authoritative DTP statement locating each beam line relative to the two bridge carriageways and identifying covered lanes.
3. DTP definition of exported `heading` values and resolution of contrary heading records (including the prior 504 W sample and 505 E samples).
4. Independent validation that the versioned SUMO segment/lane geometry represents the same road section and lane configuration as the installation period.

## Provenance and claim boundary

Research supported claims: current public DTP labels, coordinates, published dataset schema/aggregation, official Vicmap dataset scope, and official corridor configuration facts.  
Not established: field-validated detector location, CRS/accuracy, sensor orientation, lane coverage, DTP heading semantics, or detector-to-SUMO mapping.  
Allowed wording: “DTP labels sites 504/505 as inbound/outbound West Gate Bridge sites; detector-to-network mapping remains unresolved.”  
Avoid: “504/505 are mapped to the eastbound/westbound SUMO edges” or any claim that nearest geometry establishes lane or direction.
